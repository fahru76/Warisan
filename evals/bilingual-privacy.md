# Bilingual and privacy acceptance criteria

- Both domains switch between English and Bahasa Melayu; language survives reload and updates document language and OG locale.
- Cultural terms remain unchanged.
- Optional analytics is unchecked by default; essential-only and optional choices are accessible.
- Preferences are versioned and timestamped; privacy settings can reopen and optional analytics can be withdrawn.
- Storage failures do not crash either app.
- Registration/booking PDPA consent is separate from the privacy banner and marketing opt-in.
- CI runs typechecks and both builds without live Supabase credentials.
